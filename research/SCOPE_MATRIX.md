## Scope discipline after ROUND-0001

A physically reachable witness in one model is not yet an open-family theorem.

A parameterized IFT argument is not itself novelty.

A result on arbitrary ambient histories does not establish a result on \(\mathcal R_\alpha\).

A result on \(\overline{\mathcal R_\alpha}\) does not establish physical reachability without an additional argument.
