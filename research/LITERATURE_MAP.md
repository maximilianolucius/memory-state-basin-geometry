# Literature Map — Memory-State Basin Geometry

## Direct foundations

### Doan & Kloeden (2021)
Semi-Dynamical Systems Generated by Autonomous Caputo Fractional Differential Equations.  
Vietnam Journal of Mathematics 49, 1305–1315.  
DOI 10.1007/s10013-020-00464-6.

Role: memory-state/Volterra semidynamical representation.

### Cong & Tuan (2017)
Generation of Nonlocal Fractional Dynamical Systems by Fractional Differential Equations.  
Journal of Integral Equations and Applications 29(4), 585–608.  
DOI 10.1216/JIE-2017-29-4-585.

Role: scalar nonintersection and explicit higher-dimensional trajectory intersection.

### Diethelm & Ford (2012)
Volterra Integral Equations and Fractional Calculus: Do Neighboring Solutions Intersect?  
Journal of Integral Equations and Applications 24(1), 25–37.  
DOI 10.1216/JIE-2012-24-1-25.

Role: trajectory intersection/nonintersection background.

## Strong adjacent hereditary/basin prior

### Huang, Yang, Yi & Zou (2014)
On the Basins of Attraction for a Class of Delay Differential Equations with Non-Monotone Bistable Nonlinearities.  
Journal of Differential Equations 256(7), 2101–2114.  
DOI 10.1016/j.jde.2013.12.015.

Killer test: does hereditary theory imply endpoint-fiber basin purity for the relevant Caputo reachable set?

## Comparison/monotonicity prior

### Wu (2023)
Comparison Principles for Systems of Caputo Fractional Order Ordinary Differential Equations.  
Chaos, Solitons & Fractals 171, 113437.  
DOI 10.1016/j.chaos.2023.113437.

Role: quasi-monotone and mixed-monotone comparison; possible purity regimes.

## Double/Multiple Allee baseline

### Berec, Angulo & Courchamp (2007)
Multiple Allee Effects and Population Management.  
DOI 10.1016/j.tree.2006.12.002.

### Pal & Saha (2015)
Qualitative Analysis of a Predator–Prey System with Double Allee Effect in Prey.  
DOI 10.1016/j.chaos.2014.12.007.

### Contreras Julio & Aguirre (2018)
Allee Thresholds and Basins of Attraction in a Predation Model with Double Allee Effect.  
DOI 10.1002/mma.4774.

### Rahmi et al. (2021)
A Modified Leslie–Gower Model Incorporating Beddington–DeAngelis Functional Response, Double Allee Effect and Memory Effect.  
DOI 10.3390/fractalfract5030084.

### Mondal et al. (2025)
Dynamics of a Fractional Order Predator–Prey System with Double Allee Effect and Group Defense.  
DOI 10.1016/j.cjph.2025.09.020.

Role: natural applied class with positivity, multistability, basins and incommensurate order.
