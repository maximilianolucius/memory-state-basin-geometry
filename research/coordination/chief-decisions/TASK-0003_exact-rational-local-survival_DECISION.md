## 2. PROPOSITION O1 — physical invariant survival-set obstruction

Assume X1 and let \(S\subset X_{\rm phys}\) satisfy:

1. every canonical cold start in \(S\) is survival-bound,
   \[
   \iota(S)\subseteq\mathcal B(E^*);
   \]
2. for a standard initial point \(p\in S\), its physical orbit remains in \(S\).

Then that orbit cannot enter
\[
R_{\rm ext}.
\]

Indeed X1 gives
\[
\iota(R_{\rm ext})\subseteq\mathcal B(0),
\]
so
\[
S\cap R_{\rm ext}=\varnothing
\]
because the two asymptotic limits are distinct.

For THEOREM-L1, its certified ellipsoid \(\Omega\) satisfies both hypotheses:
- every cold start in \(\Omega\) converges to \(E^*\);
- the Lyapunov comparison keeps the physical orbit inside \(\Omega\).

Therefore:
\[
\boxed{\Omega\cap R_{\rm ext}=\varnothing}
\]
and no L1-certified orbit can ever produce the target threshold entry.

### Consequence

The target phenomenon cannot be certified by a forward-invariant region of the **present physical state** whose cold starts all have the survival label.

A successful survival proof must retain history/memory information or otherwise certify a non-forward-invariant physical initial-value set.

This is not a failure of THEOREM-L1; it is a consequence of the phenomenon being genuinely memory-state dependent.

## 5. PROPOSITION O2 — obstruction to the tested global overshoot certificate

For Hurwitz \(J\), consider the Stage-E certificate based on:
- a weighted max norm with diagonal weights;
- the global variation-of-constants kernel
  \[
  \Psi(t)=t^{\alpha-1}E_{\alpha,\alpha}(Jt^\alpha);
  \]
- one global radius \(r\);
- the crude product bound \(K C(r)r^2\).

If that certificate could accommodate threshold entry, a necessary condition would imply
\[
\frac{4g c_2}{x^*(1-x^*)}<1,
\]
where
\[
g=x^*-\theta,\qquad c_2=3x^*-1-\theta.
\]

But Hurwitz stability gives
\[
x^*>\frac{1+\theta}{2},
\]
hence
\[
g>\frac{1-\theta}{2},\qquad
c_2>\frac{1+\theta}{2},
\]
and
\[
x^*(1-x^*)<\frac{1-\theta^2}{4}.
\]

Therefore
\[
\frac{4g c_2}{x^*(1-x^*)}>4.
\]

Contradiction.

Thus this **specific global certificate family** is impossible in the Hurwitz regime, for every \(0<\alpha<1\) and every diagonal weight.

Do not generalize O2 to arbitrary memory-dependent certificates or to the non-Hurwitz regime.

The proof target is now a **memory-tail certificate**, not a physical-state basin radius.
