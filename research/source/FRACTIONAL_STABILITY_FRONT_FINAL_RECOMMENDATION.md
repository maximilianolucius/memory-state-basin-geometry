## Principal research opportunity

### Observable insufficiency and basin ambiguity induced by fractional memory

Consider an autonomous multidimensional Caputo system represented on its physically meaningful memory state.

Let:
- (mathcal R_alpha) be the reachable memory-state set generated by standard physical Caputo IVPs;
- (e_0) be the present-state evaluation map;
- (mathcal B(A)) denote the basin of an asymptotic state (A).

For a currently observed physical state (x), define

[
mathcal F_x
=
e_0^{-1}(x)capmathcal R_alpha.
]

The central research question is:

[
oxed{
	ext{Can }
mathcal F_x
	ext{ intersect two different asymptotic basins?}
}
]

For the preferred ecological realization:

[
oxed{
exists phi,psiinmathcal F_x:
quad
phiinmathcal B(A_{m extinction}),
qquad
psiinmathcal B(A_{m survival}).
}
]

In words:

> **Can two populations with exactly the same currently observed densities have different long-run fates solely because their reachable fractional-memory states are different?**

## Why this opportunity survives the literature audit

The searched literature already establishes several weaker facts:

- scalar Caputo trajectories obey strong nonintersection/separation properties;
- in dimension (dge2), distinct Caputo trajectories can meet at the same physical state;
- Caputo systems admit memory-state/Volterra dynamical representations;
- hereditary and delay systems possess genuine history-space basin geometry;
- comparison principles exist for multidimensional Caputo systems;
- direct fractional Double-Allee predator–prey models already exhibit multistability and basin structure.

However, no resolving theorem was identified for the **fiberwise basin partition of the physically reachable Caputo memory state**.

The novelty claim must therefore be limited to that exact residual.

## Preferred Double-Allee connection

The preferred application is a positive multidimensional system with:
- strong/Double Allee effect;
- extinction as one asymptotic state;
- survival/coexistence as another;
- nontrivial multidimensional coupling;
- fractional memory.

A direct existing model class is provided by Mondal et al. (2025), DOI `10.1016/j.cjph.2025.09.020`, which contains Double Allee, multistability, basin calculations and incommensurate fractional orders.

This makes the bridge natural rather than cosmetic.

## Potential theorem family

A future independent research project could investigate:

1. basin-purity of reachable present-state fibers;
2. sufficient conditions for multibasin fibers;
3. impossibility results for scalar, triangular or selected monotone classes;
4. regularity/topology/multiplicity of fiber-basin intersections;
5. dependence on fractional order;
6. minimal dimension for basin ambiguity;
7. consequences for extinction inference from present population observations.

## Main unresolved risk

The current project has **not proved** that a natural Double-Allee model actually contains a multibasin fiber.

That is the first mathematical feasibility test for a future project.

Failure of that test would not necessarily destroy the whole program: a sufficiently broad basin-purity theorem for important positive classes could itself be interesting. But a constructive positive result would provide the strongest path.

## Secondary opportunity

### Robust multibasin threshold geometry

Study preservation and deformation of extinction/survival basins and separators under simultaneous perturbations in:
- ecological parameters;
- fractional orders;
- memory kernel;
- structured Multiple/Double-Allee mechanisms.

This is weaker as a novelty claim because robust persistence, viability and discriminating-kernel theory are mature adjacent fields.

## High-risk future opportunity

### Rare extinction in stochastic fractional memory

Starting from modern stochastic-Volterra Markov lifts and LDP theory, derive:
- quasipotential/action geometry;
- small-noise exit-time asymptotics;
- most-likely extinction paths;
- dependence on fractional order

for positive multistable strong/Double-Allee systems.

This is mathematically rich but has high novelty-decay risk because the stochastic-Volterra literature is advancing rapidly in 2026.

## What this project does not do

This repository stops at research-gap discovery and validation.

It does **not**:
- prove the proposed theorem;
- construct the decisive example;
- launch computations to discover a proof;
- write the resulting original-research paper.

Those activities belong to a separate future project.
