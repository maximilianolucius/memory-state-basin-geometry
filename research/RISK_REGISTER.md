# Risk Register

## R1 — hidden hereditary theorem kills novelty
severity: CRITICAL  
mitigation: hostile search in Volterra, delay, minimal-state, factor-map and output-equivalence theory before deep proof investment.

## R2 — phenomenon exists only in artificial ambient states
severity: CRITICAL  
mitigation: state results on \(\mathcal R_\alpha\) or a rigorously justified closure and track physical reachability.

## R3 — monotonicity/competitiveness forces purity
severity: HIGH  
mitigation: treat as a possible theorem and identify exact sign classes.

## R4 — only one numerical example exists
severity: CRITICAL  
mitigation: generalize to structural conditions/open families before manuscript writing.

## R5 — finite-time basin misclassification
severity: HIGH  
mitigation: horizon sensitivity, trapping neighborhoods and rigorous bounds where possible.

## R6 — solver memory error
severity: CRITICAL  
mitigation: history-retaining methods, independent solver cross-checks, known-solution tests.

## R7 — Double-Allee connection becomes decorative
severity: HIGH  
mitigation: the ecological model must instantiate the theorem mechanism directly.

## R8 — observability overclaim
severity: HIGH  
mitigation: distinguish asymptotic-outcome sufficiency, state observability and parameter identifiability.

## R9 — recent unpublished work erodes novelty
severity: MEDIUM/HIGH  
mitigation: monitor internally, but obey the published-only final citation rule.
