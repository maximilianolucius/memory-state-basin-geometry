## Structural candidates

### CANDIDATE-M1 — collision-to-multibasin lift
status: OPEN — analytic proof sketch complete; source hypotheses not yet audited  
statement:
If standard physical IVPs (p,q) belong, after canonical lifting, to distinct asymptotic basins and there exist ages (t,s\ge0) such that
[
x(t;p)=x(s;q)=x,
]
then
[
T_t\iota(p),T_s\iota(q)\in\mathcal F_x
]
and the two reachable continuation states remain in their respective basins by forward invariance. Hence (mathcal F_x) is multibasin.

scope: abstract memory-state semidynamical system generated by standard Caputo IVPs, subject to exact audited hypotheses  
dependencies:
- exact Doan–Kloeden continuation-state architecture;
- forward invariance of the basin definition used;
- canonical lift of standard IVPs.
evidence: analytic reduction only  
novelty: not claimed; ROUND-0001 hostile audit dispatched.
