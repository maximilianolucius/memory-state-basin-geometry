# Project Charter — Memory-State Basin Geometry

## Domain
Applied mathematics at the intersection of fractional differential equations, infinite-dimensional dynamical systems, Volterra/hereditary equations, basin geometry, positive population dynamics, and strong/Double-Allee effects.

## Central research object
For a multidimensional autonomous Caputo system, define the physically reachable memory-state set \(\mathcal R_\alpha\), present-state evaluation \(e_0\), and fibers
\[
\mathcal F_x=e_0^{-1}(x)\cap\mathcal R_\alpha.
\]
The project asks when such fibers are basin-pure and when they can be multibasin.

## Preferred applied realization
A positive multidimensional strong/Double-Allee system with:
- an extinction attractor;
- a survival/coexistence attractor;
- nontriangular coupling;
- a genuine memory-state formulation.

The application is preferred but may not weaken the theorem.

## Success criterion
Produce a theorem family of strong Q1 applied-mathematics quality, ideally combining:
- basin-purity/impossibility results for important subclasses;
- existence of multibasin fibers under explicit hypotheses;
- topology/regularity/multiplicity of fiber-basin intersections;
- dependence on fractional order;
- a direct Double-Allee specialization;
- rigorous computation that corroborates but never replaces proof.

## Non-objectives
This is not another local-stability paper, another basin heat map, a generic claim that memory matters, an alpha-dependent scalar equilibrium-threshold paper, or a manuscript written before the theorem exists.

## Publication standard
The workflow is governed by agent_directives_publishable_first_submission.md: theorem first, <=25 pages, self-contained, published references only in the submitted paper, no essential supplement, and a strong theorem-driven visual program.
